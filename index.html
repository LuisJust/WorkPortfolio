Projects

HVAC for the kitchen & canteen of a senior residence – Porto

Final-year project (PESTM) · ISEP · 2023

Design of an all-air heating and ventilation system, backed by a reversible heat pump, for the canteen and commercial kitchen of a senior residence. The building was modelled in Revit and the full mechanical installation was implemented in the BIM model.

  • Air-flow design to EN 15251 and Portuguese regulations: canteen supply 1,750 m³/h (25 occupants), kitchen extraction 3,100 m³/h with make-up air through two hoods
  • Duct sizing and pressure-loss calculation on critical paths (185 Pa / 274 Pa); selection of two Systemair Geniox AHUs
  • Fire-safe kitchen extraction: F400 fan (400 °C / 120 min) and fire-resistant duct insulation
  • Heating loop: Daikin EWYT050 air-to-water heat pump (49.5 kW), inertia tank, expansion vessel, Grundfos circulator and pipe sizing
  • Bill of quantities and 15 drawing sheets produced from the Revit model

Solar domestic hot water system for a house – Arouca

2nd-year design project · ISEP · 2022

Design of a solar thermal system with a condensing gas boiler as backup, producing domestic hot water for a two-storey single-family house, from climate analysis to the full Revit MEP model and drawings.

  • Demand of 160 L/day; site irradiation from PVGIS and performance simulation in SCE.ER
  • Forced-circulation system: 2 compact solar collectors (3.88 m²), 300 L storage tank and condensing boiler – 69% solar fraction
  • Primary circuit sizing, pressure losses, pump and expansion-vessel selection; distribution pipework to DIN 1988-300
  • Annual energy and gas-cost estimate for the backup system

Tire cleaning station – mechanical design

Mechanical design project · ISEP · 2023

Reverse-engineering and redesign of an industrial station that holds and rotates tires (14" to 24" rims) while a laser robot cleans their inner surface. Designed in SolidWorks using real supplier components.

  • Six radial support fingers driven by rack-and-pinion gearboxes (Leantechnik Lifgo), linked by universal joints
  • Drive: Mitsubishi servo motor with a 1:10 Wittenstein alpha gearbox and an HTD 5M timing-belt stage
  • Torque/power calculation for motor selection and bending checks in FTOOL to choose plate material and size
  • Full assembly, part drawings and bill of materials with SKF bearings and standard components
